## Reeli (scenariji)

### Reel A — »Shoes by the door« (≤ 7 s, posnameš s telefonom)
- **Posnetki:** (1) otroški čevlji pri vratih, roka jih zgrabi · (2) povodec se sname s kljuke · (3) vrata se odprejo · (4) noge na poti / tačke na travi. Otroci samo od zadaj ali samo noge.
- **Napis od 1. sličice:** `She said: "Lahko bi šli walk your pet"` → pri vratih: `#walkyourpet is a thing now.` → zadnja 1 s: endcard.
- **Opis:** `A kid said it. Now it's a movement. 🐾 Show us your walk — shoes, leash, paws (no faces) — with #walkyourpet. Who's walking today? 👇` · `#walkyourpet #dogtok #momtok #petprep #dogwalk`

### Reel B — Curio »walk your pet?« (≤ 7 s)
- Nadgradnja epizode 2 (`brand/mascot/STORIES.md`). Prompte napiše Claude, **generira David** (najprej slika v Nano Banana Pro, šele potem video).
- **Slika:** character block + `Scene: a cozy, bright modern living room in soft afternoon light. The mascot is fast asleep on a round mint-green dog bed in the lower half of the frame, eyelids fully closed as two soft curved lines. Behind it, slightly out of focus, a white front door with a mint leash hanging on a hook. Close full-body shot from a low angle at the mascot's level, a calm bare wall above. Stylized 3D render, premium designer vinyl toy look, soft diffused light, clean and uncluttered.` (9:16)
- **Video:** character block + `It sleeps peacefully for half a second. Then its eyes snap wide open, pupils tiny with excitement. It hops upright out of the bed and scurries toward the door, the round tag swinging, and stops under the leash on the hook, looking up at it. Small, springy motion. Static camera at low eye level, soft afternoon light, premium 3D designer vinyl toy animation, one single character, clean frame.`
- **Napis od 1. sličice:** `Me: *whispers* "walk your pet?"` → endcard.
- **Opis:** `Say it once. Every pet. Every time. 😂 Does yours do this?` · `#walkyourpet #dogtok #funnypets #petprep #momtok`

### Reel C — sitcom skeč (≤ 15 s)
- **Prizor:** starš na kavču, otrok(a) s prijateljico. Starš: `"Go play outside for once."` Otroka: `"Nah."` Prijateljica tiho: `"Lahko bi šli walk your pet."` Rez: oba že stojita pri vratih s čevlji in povodcem. Starš strmi v kamero. → endcard
- **Opis:** `Three words did what 100 "go outside"s couldn't. 😂` · `#walkyourpet #momtok #dogtok #parenting #petprep`
- Igralci: odrasli ali otroci s soglasjem staršev; telefon nikoli ni glavni junak.
