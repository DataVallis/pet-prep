# PetPrep pes v resničnem prostoru — reeli (Simulon-slog)

*Odločitev David 2026-11-06:* navdih je Simulon »Reality-Aware Rendering« (AR lik postaviš v pravi prostor, posnameš, AI posnetek na novo zgenerira, da lik reagira na roko, glas in okolico). **Zdaj samo marketing**, funkcija v aplikaciji kasneje (glej spodaj »Za kasneje«).

## Pravila
- **Prompte samo napišem, generiraš ti** (Higgsfield): najprej ena sličica/kratek test, šele potem celoten klip, da ne zapravimo kreditov za neposkušen kader.
- Pes = **realističen PetPrep pes** (isti look kot v aplikaciji, npr. Labradorec ali mešanček), **ne Curio**.
- **Ne obljubljamo funkcije, ki je v aplikaciji ni.** Reel ne sme reči »aplikacija postavi psa v tvojo sobo«. Sporočilo je: to je AI pes, ki ga otrok 12 tednov skrbi, preden dobi pravega.
- Oznaka **»AI-generated«** v kotu (TikTok/Meta zahtevata oznako za realistično AI vsebino) + nastavitev »AI-generated content« ob objavi.
- **Brez otroških obrazov**: snemamo roko, noge, čevlje, ali odraslega. Otrok ni prilepljen na telefon.
- Endcard kot vedno: Curio + follow @petprep.si + App Store / Google Play »coming soon«. Največ 5 hashtagov, EN.
- Shranjeno v Drive po `MEDIA_DRIVE.md` (CGP → videji → Real room).

## Kako snemaš (pomembno za kakovost)
1. Na mesto psa postavi **plišastega psa podobne velikosti** (ne prazen zrak): roka se ga dejansko dotika, zato AI dobi pravilno senco, prekrivanje in pritisk na blazino.
2. iPhone, 4K/30, vodoravno ali 9:16, **mirna kamera** (stativ ali naslonjen telefon), dobra dnevna svetloba, 5–8 s.
3. Gibi počasni in jasni (božanje po glavi, praskanje za ušesom); nič hitrih rezov.
4. Kot referenco pošlji portret pasme s petprep.si (ali key image konkretnega psa iz admina) — isti pes kot v aplikaciji.
5. Pot A: Higgsfield **Genjutsu → Replace object** (video + referenčna slika + prompt spodaj). Pot B (test): aplikacija **Simulon** (iPhone) — preveriti, ali dovoli uvoz lastnega lika.

## Reel 1 — »He's not real« (≤ 8 s)
- **Posnetek:** dnevna soba, plišasti pes leži na kavču. Odrasla roka pride v kader in ga boža po glavi, nato praska za ušesom.
- **Replace prompt:** `Replace the plush toy on the sofa with the real dog from the reference image: a photorealistic yellow Labrador Retriever, same coat colour and face as the reference. The dog lies relaxed on the sofa cushion, and when the hand touches its head it leans into the hand, closes its eyes halfway and its tail thumps softly on the cushion. Correct contact shadows, fur reacting to the fingers, cushion pressed under its weight. Keep the room, lighting, hand and camera exactly as in the original video. Photorealistic, natural motion, no extra animals, no text.`
- **Napis od 1. sličice:** `This is Max. He's not real (yet).` → zadnje 2 s: `My kid raises him for 12 weeks first.` → endcard.
- **Opis:** `Before we get a real dog, there's Max. 12 weeks of feeding, walks and care — then we'll know. 🐾` · `#petprep #dogtok #momtok #walkyourpet #aidog`

## Reel 2 — #walkyourpet pri vratih (≤ 8 s)
- **Posnetek:** vhodna vrata, plišasti pes sedi na predpražniku. V kader pridejo otroški čevlji (samo noge), roka sname povodec s kljuke.
- **Replace prompt:** `Replace the plush dog by the door with the real dog from the reference image, photorealistic, same coat colour and face. The dog sits by the door, and as the shoes step in and the leash comes off the hook its ears perk up, it stands, spins once and wags its whole back end, looking up at the leash. Correct floor shadows and natural paw placement. Keep the hallway, feet, leash, lighting and camera exactly as in the original video. Photorealistic, no extra animals, no text.`
- **Napis:** `Me: "walk your pet?"` → endcard.
- **Opis:** `Say it once. Every pet. Every time. 😂 Who's walking today? 👇` · `#walkyourpet #dogtok #momtok #petprep #dogwalk`

## Reel 3 — sitcom (slog epizod 4 in 7, ≤ 15 s)
- **Prizor:** oče bere na kavču, plišasti pes ob njem. Otrok (od zadaj) zakliče iz kuhinje: `"Did Max eat yet?"` Oče: `"Max is not real."` Pes (zamenjan) počasi obrne glavo proti očetu in ga užaljeno gleda. Oče: `"...He ate at 7."`
- **Replace prompt:** `Replace the plush dog next to the man on the sofa with the real dog from the reference image, photorealistic, same coat colour and face. At first it rests calmly; after the man speaks it slowly turns its head toward him with an offended, unimpressed look, ears slightly back, then lets out a small sigh. Keep the people, room, lighting and camera exactly as in the original video. Photorealistic, natural motion, no extra animals, no text.`
- **Napis:** podnapisi dialoga → endcard.
- **Opis:** `He's AI. He still has feelings. 😂` · `#petprep #momtok #dogtok #walkyourpet #familycomedy`
- Vesela družinska komedija (brez jeze); igralci z dovoljenjem, otrok brez obraza ali z soglasjem staršev.
